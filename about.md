# About

**SciML Weekly** is a once-a-week digest of new methods in scientific machine learning. It focuses on neural operators,
physics foundation models, statistical learning for dynamical systems, AI weather models, and ML surrogates for
computational fluid dynamics.

Each issue aims to be **detailed but easy to read**:

- A short **TL;DR table** at the top, so you can skim.
- A **concept of the week** that explains one idea from first principles.
- For each paper: *what's the idea*, *what's new*, *what the results are*, and *why it matters*.
- Tables and figures where they make things clearer. Figures are either redrawn by me (and labelled as such) or used
  with credit to the original source.

## Sourcing rules

1. Every factual claim links to a primary source: the paper, official release notes, or the organisation's own announcement.
2. Numbers are quoted from the source, not estimated. Company-reported benchmarks are labelled as such.
3. Papers I haven't read closely are listed as titles only, never summarised.
4. Dates in the [model timeline](releases.html) are first public release dates (preprint or announcement).
